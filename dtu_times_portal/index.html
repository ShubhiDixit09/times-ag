From 25 Chaotic WhatsApp Groups to 1 Unified Edition Portal

How the new platform solves editorial bottlenecks, automates Google Drive storage, enforces multi-tier quality reviews, and provides concrete activity telemetry for year-end appraisals.

Current Friction (25+ WhatsApp Groups)

  • Massive Associate Burnout: 9 associate editors must monitor 20–25 chats, chasing article deadlines manually every hour.
  • Asset Compression & Loss: Photos and artwork shared on WhatsApp lose print resolution and get scattered in phone galleries.
  • Version Chaos: Designers are unsure if a paragraph sent at 2:00 AM on WhatsApp is the final sign-off or an unapproved draft.
  • Unverifiable Appraisals: Year-end leadership selections rely on vague recall or Google Forms where members exaggerate contributions.

The Edition Portal Architecture

  • Single Source of Truth: One clean web portal. Standard recurring sections auto-populate autonomously on edition creation.
  • Automated Google Drive Scaffolding: API provisions structured folders (`/Columnists`, `/Illustrations`, `/Photos`) with email permissions.
  • 5-Stage Editorial Gatekeeper: Draft → Associate Review → EIC Polish → Design Pairing → Print-Ready. Zero ambiguity.
  • Concrete Activity Tracker: Tracks every submission timestamp, turnaround speed, and quality score for clear end-of-year appraisals.
